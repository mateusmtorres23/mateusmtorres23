## 💼 Software for public service

### FarmaGestor · Prefeitura de Lagoa do Ouro

**Volunteer Development Intern · July 2025 – April 2026**

I worked alongside another student developer on a web application for **pharmaceutical inventory management** at the municipality of Lagoa do Ouro, Pernambuco. The system supports stock organization, inventory movements, and medication expiration tracking.

My contributions included:

- Developing the REST API and business logic with **Django and Django REST Framework**.
- Integrating the application with **PostgreSQL**.
- Building the **React** interface and connecting it to the backend.
- Maintaining and adapting features after the municipality's IT team deployed the application.

This was my first professional software project: an opportunity to collaborate on an application that moved into production and required follow-up changes.

`Python` `Django REST Framework` `React` `PostgreSQL`

## 🔬 Research & teaching

### Computational modeling of BRT systems

**PIBIC/CNPq undergraduate research scholarship · UPE · Since September 2026**

I'm investigating how fare evasion may affect the operational sustainability of Bus Rapid Transit systems, particularly the relationship between lost revenue, maintenance resources, failures, and fleet availability.

The project involves **stochastic modeling, Stochastic Petri Nets, Monte Carlo simulation, and Python** to study these relationships.

### Machine learning for anti-money laundering

**Faculty-supervised research group · UPE · Since June 2026**

I participate in weekly research meetings and experiments exploring potentially suspicious patterns in financial transactions. My work includes **exploratory analysis, data preparation, feature engineering, and experiments with unsupervised models**.

The research repository contains studies using IBM transaction data and temporal, behavioral, and graph-based representations of transaction activity.

**[↗ Explore AML Research](https://github.com/mateusmtorres23/aml-research)**

### Calculus I teaching assistant

**UPE · Third semester of my degree**

I supported students through individual questions, study sessions, and lessons and reviews as needed. The role gave me practice breaking down mathematical ideas, listening to where students were struggling, and adapting my explanations.
